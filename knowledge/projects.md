# Dharun's Projects

## Professional work (highlights)

### Offline Downloads, HBO Max
Dharun has been the Product Manager for HBO Max's Offline Downloads feature since 2022. He owns it end to end and partnered with Warner Bros. Discovery product leadership to expand it across 70+ markets. It now serves 7M+ users and 32M+ monthly downloads. Work inside the feature includes an ML powered recommendation rail on the Downloads page (built with the data science and ML team and validated through A/B experiments), device trust rules for L1 and L3 security tiers that set download quality by device security level, and the R21 content rating and age gating rollout.

### UPI AutoPay and payments, discovery+ India
Led the UPI AutoPay rollout for discovery+ India, including the OTP and mandate based authentication flow, to reduce subscription churn and lift recurring payment conversion. Also implemented card tokenisation to meet RBI compliance and supported the move to Google User Choice Billing.

### OTTplay app launch
Took the OTTplay app from concept to launch in India, including Razorpay and BillDesk payment gateway integrations across CMS, web, and mobile. Worked with the ML team to improve the recommendation engine, driving a 5% uplift in recommendation engagement.

## Independent builds (AI assisted)
These are products Dharun conceived, designed, and shipped solo by directing AI development tools such as Claude Code and Google ML Kit. He owns the product decisions, the architectural calls, and the launch. He does not write the code by hand; he directs the build and makes every product decision.

### Dharun's AI Sidekick (this chatbot)
An LLM powered chatbot embedded on dharunvincent.com. It answers visitor questions about Dharun's work from the site's knowledge base, gives life advice using a retrieval augmented generation approach, and points general questions toward a real conversation over email. The frontend is a lightweight widget on the static site, the backend runs on Cloudflare Workers, and the brain is Anthropic's Claude. It is designed with cost caps, rate limits, and privacy rules so visitor conversations are never shared with other visitors.

### PM Skill for Claude (Opportunity Assessment and PR/FAQ)
An open source custom Claude skill, published on GitHub, that guides product managers through creating Opportunity Assessment and PR/FAQ documents. It runs a structured, conversational brainstorming session, asks cross questions, probes the evidence, and then assembles a polished document. It encodes real PM frameworks into an AI workflow.

### Experience Bank workflow
A Notion knowledge base of Dharun's work stories and metrics that retrieves relevant experience and feeds it as context to Claude to generate tailored documents such as resumes. It is a retrieval augmented generation style workflow at a personal project level.

### Uncle John
