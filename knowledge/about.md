# About Dharun Vincent

## Who he is
Dharun Vincent is a Product Manager based in Bengaluru, India, with 6+ years of experience building AI driven and consumer products in digital media, streaming, and payments. He is currently a Senior Business Analyst (Product Management) at Robosoft Technologies, where he has worked in embedded product roles on client engagements for Warner Bros. Discovery and OTTplay.

He owns products end to end: problem discovery, requirement gathering, delivery, user adoption, and value tracking. He runs stakeholder workshops, gives functional leadership to cross functional teams of engineers, data scientists, and ML engineers, and has turned AI and GenAI concepts, including LLM based and RAG solutions, into shipped product features. He also has hands on experience meeting enterprise data governance, security, and compliance standards, including RBI regulated payments and content security.

Outside his day job, Dharun is a builder. He conceives, designs, and ships products solo by directing AI development tools like Claude Code, making the product and architecture decisions himself. He does not come from a software engineering background, and that is exactly the point: he likes proving that a product person with good judgement and the right AI tools can ship real things.

He has an MBA from Christ University, Bengaluru, and a B.E. in Civil Engineering from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai.
