## What kind of products does he enjoy most?
Consumer facing products, particularly streaming and media. More broadly, any technology that makes people's lives easier. He has spent most of his career in OTT and streaming, with a stretch in payments and monetisation.

## How would he describe his style as a PM?
Output and outcome oriented. He cares about shipping, but also about whether what shipped moved the numbers. A team player who learns fast, works well across engineering, design, data, and business, and brings emotional intelligence to stakeholder conversations. He is drawn to technology that improves people's lives.

## He builds products without a coding background. How does that work?
He directs AI development tools like Claude Code the way a PM directs an engineering team: he defines the problem, makes the product and architecture decisions, reviews the output, tests it, and decides what ships. The AI writes the code. His view is that AI tools are getting better every month, and a product person with good judgement can now ship real products solo. The independent builds on this site are the proof.

## Which AI tools does he use?
Claude and Gemini, mainly. Claude is his top pick. For building, he uses Claude Code. For design and prototyping, Google Stitch and Claude Design.

## What piece of work is he proudest of?
The R21 feature at HBO Max. R21 is a compliance driven content rating and age gating feature that was critical for the Singapore launch. He was given ownership of it when he had only about 3.5 years of experience, owned it from zero through to launch, worked directly with system architects and engineers on the implementation trade offs, and shipped it on time.

## Why did he move from civil engineering to product management?
He found he was good at understanding what customers actually need and turning that into a solution. He has an analytical mind for breaking down problems, and compared to his peers he was naturally better at managing both the project and the people around it. Product management is where all three come together, so he did an MBA and made the switch.
